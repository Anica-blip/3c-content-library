Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026  ·  Applies to: 3c-public-library.org

👤 Who We Are

The 3C Public Library is operated by 3C Thread To Success, a personal development and leadership training brand. Our platform provides educational content, interactive presentations, and learning resources to our community.

For any privacy-related questions or requests, you can contact us at:
📧 3c.innertherapy@gmail.com

📋 What Data We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to operate the library and serve our community.

  • Comments: When you post a comment, we collect your name and optionally your email address. Your email is never displayed publicly.
  • Usage data: Standard web server logs may record your IP address, browser type, and pages visited. This data is used solely for security and performance purposes and is not shared with third parties.
  • Session data: When you access the library, a session cookie is set to keep you authenticated. No personal profile is created automatically.

We do not collect payment data, run advertising trackers, or sell any personal data to third parties.

🎯 How We Use Your Data

  • To display your comments on the content you engage with
  • To maintain your session so you do not need to log in repeatedly
  • To keep the library secure and monitor for abuse
  • To improve content and understand how resources are used (anonymised)

We process this data on the legal basis of legitimate interest (running and securing the platform) and consent (optional cookies and comments you choose to submit).

🗄️ Data Storage & Third Parties

Your data is stored using the following services:

  • Supabase — database and authentication provider. Data is stored on servers within the EU/US under Supabase's Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare — content delivery, storage (R2), and Workers. Governed by Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare Pages — hosting platform for this website, in addition to Cloudflare's CDN, Workers, and R2 storage listed above. All governed by Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

None of these providers sell your data. We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any advertising trackers.

🕐 How Long We Keep Your Data

  • Comments: Kept until you request deletion or the content is removed.
  • Session cookies: Expire when you close your browser or within 7 days.
  • Server logs: Automatically deleted after 30 days.

⚖️ Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are in the EU or UK, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access — request a copy of data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification — ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure — ask us to delete your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restrict processing — ask us to limit how we use your data
  • Right to object — object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Right to data portability — request your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, email us at 3c.innertherapy@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

🇪🇺 You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. In the UK this is the ICO. In the EU, find your authority at edpb.europa.eu.

🍪 Cookie Policy

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We use the following cookies on the 3C Public Library:

Cookie Type Purpose Expires
sb-* Essential Supabase authentication session. Required for the library to function. 7 days
3c_cookie_consent Essential Stores your cookie preference so the banner does not show on every visit. 1 year
__cf_* Essential Cloudflare security and performance cookies. Help protect against bots. Session
Functional storage Functional Browser localStorage used to remember your preferences (e.g. zoom level in the viewer). No personal data is stored. Until cleared

You can manage or withdraw your cookie consent at any time using the Cookie Settings link in the page footer, or by clearing your browser cookies.

🧒 Children's Privacy

The 3C Public Library is intended for users aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16. If you believe a child has submitted data to us, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

🔄 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the library after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
