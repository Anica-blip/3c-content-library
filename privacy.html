Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026  ·  Applies to: 3c-public-library.org (the Main Library and Aurion Vault)

👤 Who We Are

The 3C Public Library and Aurion Vault are run by 3C Thread To Success™, a personal development and leadership brand based in Portugal. The person responsible for your personal data (the "data controller") is Anabela Da Guerra.

The site is free to use. It has no shop, takes no payments and holds no financial information.

For any privacy question or request, email: 3c.innertherapy@gmail.com

📋 What Data We Collect

Visitors do not need an account, and nobody has to sign in to read the library. We collect only what the features you choose to use need.

  • Comments (on pages that offer a comment box): your name, your comment and, only if you choose to give it, your email address. Your email address is never shown to other visitors and cannot be read by them. Only the site owner can see it. Comments appear on the page straight away, and we may remove any comment.
  • Drop In chat (Aurion Vault): the message you type and which persona you chose to write to. The chat does not ask for your name or email address, so please do not put personal details in your message.
  • Reviews (Aurion Vault): your emoji or star rating, which part of the site you are reviewing, an optional written note (up to 1,000 characters) and, if you choose, whether you are a visitor or a member. Reviews do not ask for your name or email address. A review appears publicly only after the site owner approves it.
  • Technical data: like every website, the services that deliver this site receive your IP address and browser type when your device asks for a page or file. We do not use this to build profiles of visitors.

We do not collect payment data, run advertising trackers or analytics services, or sell personal data to anyone.

🔐 Members' Area (Not Yet Open)

A password-protected members' area is planned but is not open yet. When it opens, members will create an account with an email address and password, and will confirm their email address. Opening content there may count a view and record which content was opened, the page reached, the time spent and the browser type. We will update this policy before the members' area opens.

🎯 How We Use Your Data and Why

  • To show your comment on the page you commented on
  • To read your Drop In message or review and, if we choose, reply or publish it
  • To keep the site secure and to deal with abuse

Our legal basis is your consent for comments, chat messages and reviews you choose to send, and our legitimate interest in running and securing the site. You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting us.

🗄️ Who Handles Your Data

These services process data on our behalf, or are contacted by your browser, when you use the site:

  • Supabase: our database, which holds comments and the library listings. Our database is located in the EU (Stockholm). Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare: hosts the website, stores the library files, and stores Drop In chats and reviews. It also provides security protection. Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • Telegram: when someone sends a Drop In message or a review, a notification containing the start of that message is sent to the site owner's private Telegram account. Telegram Privacy Policy.
  • jsDelivr and Cloudflare cdnjs: some pages load open-source code (the database connection, PDF viewer, flipbook and video player) from these public delivery networks. Your browser contacts them directly, so they can see your IP address.
  • Google Fonts: the sign-in page only. The rest of the site uses fonts stored on our own servers.
  • YouTube and Vimeo: only on content that embeds a video from them. When you play such a video, that service may receive your IP address and set its own cookies. Their own privacy policies apply.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. They describe the safeguards they use, such as standard contractual clauses, in their own policies.

🕐 How Long We Keep Your Data

  • Comments: kept until you ask us to delete them or we remove them.
  • Drop In chats and reviews: kept until the site owner deletes them. A chat that is deleted, or a review that is rejected, is permanently erased automatically 7 days later.
  • Telegram notifications: stay in the site owner's Telegram account until they are deleted.
  • Technical data: handled by Cloudflare and Supabase under their own policies.

⚖️ Your Rights (GDPR)

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the equivalent UK law, you have these rights over your personal data:

  • Right of access: ask for a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification: ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure: ask us to delete your data
  • Right to restrict processing: ask us to limit how we use your data
  • Right to object: object to processing based on our legitimate interest
  • Right to data portability: ask for your data in a portable format
  • Right to withdraw consent: at any time, without affecting what was done before

To use any of these rights, email 3c.innertherapy@gmail.com. We will reply within one month. Because comments, chats and reviews are not linked to an account, please tell us the name you used and roughly when and where you wrote it, so that we can find it.

🇪🇺 You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In Portugal this is the National Data Protection Commission (CNPD). In another EU country, find your authority at edpb.europa.eu. In the UK it is the ICO.

🍪 Cookies and Browser Storage

We set no advertising or analytics cookies. The site stores a few small items in your browser to work properly:

Item Type Purpose Kept
3c_cookie_consent Essential Remembers your answer to the cookie banner, so it does not show on every visit. Until you clear it
dropin_last_thread Functional Remembers your latest Drop In chat, so you can return to it. It contains a random chat code, not your name. Until you clear it or the chat is deleted
aurion_* and pdfZoomLevel Functional Remember small preferences, such as whether a welcome message has already been shown today and your PDF zoom level. No personal data is stored. Until you clear it
sb-* Essential Keeps you signed in. Used only by the site owner today, and by members once the members' area opens. Ordinary visitors never receive it. Until you sign out
Cloudflare security Essential Cloudflare may set security cookies to protect the site against bots and attacks. Set by Cloudflare

Because we use no advertising or analytics cookies, the cookie banner records your choice and nothing on the site depends on it. YouTube and Vimeo videos, where a page includes them, may set their own cookies when you play them. You can change your choice at any time using Cookie Settings in the page footer, or clear your browser's stored data.

🛡️ Keeping Your Data Safe

Connections to the site are encrypted. Only the site owner can add, change or delete library content, and the email addresses given with comments cannot be read by other visitors.

🧒 Children's Privacy

The site is intended for people aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.
