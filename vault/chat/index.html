💬 How Drop In Works

What is Drop In?

Drop In is a simple way to write to Aurion, Caelum, or Anica. Every reply is written by a real person, so you'll always receive a genuine response rather than an automated one.

How do I see my reply?

As soon as you send your message, Drop In creates a private link just for your conversation.
This device will remember that link automatically, but we recommend saving it yourself or using the Copy Link button. That way you'll always have an easy way to return whenever you like.

I lost my link. What should I do?

No problem.
Simply get in touch with us again and let us know roughly what you asked about and when you sent your message. We'll do our best to locate your conversation and send the link back to you.

Is my conversation stored forever?

No.
Your conversations aren't connected to your identity unless you choose to share your details. Once your conversation has naturally come to an end, it will be permanently removed.

Am I chatting with AI?

No.
Every message is read and every reply is written by a real person. That means responses may take a little longer than an automated chat, but you'll receive a thoughtful reply from someone who has actually taken the time to read what you've shared.

Who should I write to?

Choose the person whose style feels right for you.

  • Aurion — Friendly encouragement, curiosity, and a gentle nudge in the right direction.
  • Caelum — Clear guidance, thoughtful perspective, and practical advice.
  • Anica — Honest conversations, personal insight, and a down-to-earth approach to growth.

There isn't a right or wrong choice. Simply Drop In and start the conversation with whoever you'd like to hear from.
